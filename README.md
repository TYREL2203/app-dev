# My Favorite Movie: 365 Days

**365 Days** (Polish: *365 Dni*) is an intense romantic drama movie released in 2020. The story follows a young Warsaw woman named Laura Biel who is captured by a dominant Sicilian mafia boss named Massimo Torricelli. He gives her exactly 365 days to fall in love with him while keeping her at his luxury estate.

### Why This Movie Stands Out
- **Fast-Paced Plot:** The high-stakes conflict keeps the audience guessing from beginning to end.
- **Beautiful Settings:** The film showcases stunning, scenic filming locations throughout Italy and Malta.
- **Global Popularity:** It became one of the most-watched movies globally on streaming platforms upon its release.
